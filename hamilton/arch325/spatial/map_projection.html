How a globe becomes a flat map

Paper cannot be wrapped smoothly around a ball, but it can be rolled into a cylinder or a cone, or laid flat. Classic projections wrap one of these surfaces around the globe, transfer the map onto it, and then unroll it. Three choices describe the result: the class (which surface), the case (whether it touches or cuts the globe) and the aspect (where it sits on the globe).
