U.S. Population Cartogram

Each state's area is partly scaled to its share of the U.S. population in the selected census year (a moderated distortion that keeps states recognizable), so states swell as they grow and shrink (or vanish) as they lose share or haven't yet joined. Darker blue means a larger population; hover for population and percentage of the national total.

Data source

This data comes from the United States decennial census. Documentation for 1790–1900 is available in the U.S. Census Bureau library; data for 1910–2020 is available at census.gov.

  • 1790–1880: 1880 Census, Vol. 1, Population
  • 1890: 1890 Census, Population Part 1, Section 2
  • 1900: 1900 Census, Population of U.S. States and Territories
  • 1910–2020: census.gov, Population Change Data Table
