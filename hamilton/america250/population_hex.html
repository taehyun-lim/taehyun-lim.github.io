U.S. Population in Hexagons

Each state is built from hexagons, and every hexagon stands for a fixed number of residents, so states sprout more hexagons as their population grows. Darker blue means a larger population; hover over a state to see its population and share of the U.S. total.

Data source

This data comes from the United States decennial census. Documentation for 1790–1900 is available in the U.S. Census Bureau library; data for 1910–2020 is available at census.gov.

  • 1890: 1890 Census, Population Part 1, Section 2
  • 1900: 1900 Census, Population of U.S. States and Territories
  • 1910–2020: census.gov, Population Change Data Table
