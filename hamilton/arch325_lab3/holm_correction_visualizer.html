Why you can't just compare each p to .05

Holm Correction, Step by Step

Three pairwise Mann-Whitney comparisons from the pencil-length data. Step through how Holm turns three raw p-values into three honest ones.

Holm's trick: the smallest p-value gets the harshest penalty (×m, same as plain Bonferroni), but each larger one gets a slightly gentler penalty (×(m−1), ×(m−2), …) since fewer hypotheses are still "in play" once the more significant ones are set aside. The running-max step just keeps the sequence from un-sorting itself.
